About

they're tracking us, unplug, unplug

Hey, Giuslock here.

I'm a penetration tester with around five years of experience messing with web applications, mobile apps, cloud environments, and whatever else happens to be vulnerable.

I hold the CPTS and BSCP certifications, and I'm currently working towards the OSCP. Apparently, I haven't had enough of staring at terminals and questioning my life choices.

Outside of cybersecurity, I'm interested in geopolitics, technology, and the increasingly weird relationship between humans and machines. Especially now that AI seems determined to fill every corner of the Internet with absolute slop.

I like breaking things, figuring out how they work, and occasionally wondering what the fuck we're doing as a species.

So, what's this place about?

Honestly? Whatever catches my attention.

You'll find CTF write-ups, offensive security research, certification experiences, technical notes, and whatever I happen to learn while going down yet another rabbit hole.

But this isn't strictly a cybersecurity blog.

Sometimes I'll write about AI, geopolitics, the state of the Internet, or something that's been bothering me enough to sit down and write a few paragraphs about it.

Don't expect a content strategy, perfectly structured tutorials, or groundbreaking research every week.

Some posts might be useful. Others might just be me ranting about something.

Think of this place as a personal dumping ground for technical discoveries, questionable thoughts, and occasional existential crises.

Welcome to the mess.
